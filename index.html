How to play

Shade some cells black so that:

  • Each number counts the white cells it can see up, down, left and right, itself included. Sight stops at a black cell or the edge.
  • Black cells never touch side to side.
  • All white cells join up into one group.
  • Numbered cells stay white.
Controls
  • Tap a cell: dot, then black, then clear. Hold to shade it black straight away.
  • Drag with one finger to move the board. Hold a cell, then drag, to shade a line of cells.
  • Two fingers to zoom.
  • Tap a number to see how many cells it sees now and could see at most.
  • Mouse: left click black, right click dot, scroll wheel to zoom, middle button or Space to pan.
